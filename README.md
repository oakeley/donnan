# donnan
This simulation models the Donnan effect in the context of tissue edema, which is particularly relevant for understanding conditions like heart failure, inflammation, and various pathological conditions where fluid balance is disrupted. Here's what the simulation does:

Models two compartments:

Capillary space (blood vessels)
Interstitial space (tissue fluid)


Tracks key variables:

Volumes of both compartments
Ion concentrations (Na⁺ and Cl⁻)
Protein concentrations (treated as fixed)
Membrane potential
Osmotic pressure differences


Includes important physiological parameters:

Temperature (body temperature)
Membrane permeability
Surface area
Initial conditions based on typical physiological values


The simulation shows how:

Protein concentration differences create osmotic pressure
Ion distributions adjust according to the Donnan equilibrium
Volume shifts occur between compartments
The system evolves toward equilibrium



This could be used to:

Predict edema development under different conditions
Study the effects of altered protein concentrations
Understand the impact of membrane properties on fluid distribution
Test therapeutic interventions that affect osmotic pressure
